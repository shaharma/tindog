My Story

For over seven years, I've been practicing commercial and corporate law. I started my career handling complex transactions at leading firms, and along the way developed deep expertise in data privacy — a combination that gives me a uniquely broad perspective.

I believe a good lawyer is first and foremost a business partner. Every client gets my personal, direct attention — I don't delegate work to juniors or hide behind a team. When you call, I answer.

The intersection of strong business acumen and privacy expertise allows me to see the full picture and deliver solutions that many lawyers simply cannot.
