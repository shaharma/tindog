Corporate Law

Building the right legal framework for your company — corporate governance, regulatory compliance, and risk management that keeps your organization stable and confident.

Services

How I Can Help

  • Corporate Governance

    Establishing and implementing governance frameworks — articles of association, internal policies, and structuring relationships between company organs.

  • Board Advisory

    Advising directors and officers — fiduciary duties, proper decision-making, and preventing personal liability exposure.

  • Regulatory Compliance

    Mapping regulatory requirements, building compliance programs, and adapting to evolving regulations in Israel and internationally.

  • Corporate Restructuring

    Legal counsel for restructuring processes, changes in corporate structure, spin-offs, and mergers.

  • Shareholders' Agreements

    Drafting and negotiating shareholders' agreements, investment agreements, voting rights, and protective provisions.

  • Risk Management

    Identifying, mapping, and managing legal risks — building protection mechanisms and programs to prevent exposure.

Who This Is For

Companies That Want to Do It Right

Good corporate governance isn't a luxury — it's a business necessity. I help companies of all sizes build a framework that protects them and enables growth.

Startups

Setting up the right corporate structure from day one — articles of association, founders' agreements, and decision-making frameworks.

Growing Companies (SME)

Adapting the corporate structure to growth — adding directors, compliance procedures, and internal controls.

Large Organizations

Complex corporate advisory — restructuring, structural transactions, and advanced regulatory compliance.
