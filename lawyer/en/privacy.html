Data Privacy & DPO

Comprehensive data protection for your organization — from GDPR and Israeli regulation compliance to full outsourced DPO services. Practical counsel that ensures compliance without slowing down your business.

Services

How I Can Help

  • GDPR Compliance

    Full advisory and support for GDPR compliance — from initial mapping through to complete implementation of a compliance program.

  • Database Registration & Mapping

    Mapping databases, registration with the Database Registry, and preparing ROPA documents in accordance with legal requirements.

  • DSAR Handling

    Managing and processing data subject access requests (DSAR) — access, deletion, correction, and restriction of processing rights.

  • Privacy Impact Assessments

    Conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA) for new projects and products — identifying risks and recommending mitigations.

  • Outsourced DPO Services

    Acting as your organization's external Data Protection Officer (DPO) — ongoing oversight, reporting, and regulatory compliance.

  • Staff Training

    Tailored training sessions for teams on data privacy, information security, and risk awareness — in Hebrew and English.

Who This Is For

Organizations That Handle Personal Data

Every organization that collects, processes, or stores personal data must meet regulatory requirements. I help make compliance practical and efficient.

Startups

Building a proper privacy foundation from day one — essential for fundraising and entering international markets.

Growing Companies (SME)

Adapting privacy policies to growth — database mapping, DPIA, and appointing a DPO as required.

Large Organizations

Complex privacy management — multi-domain regulatory compliance, international data transfers, and DPO services.
